# Achievements

Achievements are cosmetic profile badges. They grant no gold, items, stats, or other rewards. Open **Achievements** on the title menu to see placeholder badges, descriptions and UTC unlock dates. Definitions live in `dungeon/achievements.py`, separate from rendering and the existing encounter rewards.

**Untouched: Lower Tower** requires floors 1–5 cleared in order in one fresh campaign, with a valid Keeper defeat, no actual damage, and no deaths/retries. New characters get a unique run ID. Level Select and explicit `--level` launches invalidate the attempt, including floor 1 replays. Tutorial uses its separate practice character; practice completion cannot award the badge. Starting a new campaign retains profile badges and queued notifications.

Damage means positive health loss at the authoritative `_hurt_player` application point, after armor and immunity. All current melee, projectile, encounter and environmental attacks use this path. Armor currently has a minimum of one damage; there are no player shields or damage-over-time systems in this build. Glacial Prison and normal hit/arrival immunity fully prevent damage and do not invalidate eligibility. Future shields must report only actual damage remaining after absorption through this same path; future damage-over-time must also use it. Healing and equipment health adjustments never erase accumulated damage. Debug tools must use `world.debug_invulnerable`, whose activation permanently invalidates this attempt; directly mutating internal state is not a supported gameplay mode.

Run identity, starting/current floor, ordered clears, damage, deaths, retries and eligibility are saved alongside the character when Save Game writes a slot. Saves without complete evidence are ineligible. New slot snapshots restore the exact encounter and evidence; legacy character-only saves rebuild the room without resetting eligibility. Duplicate clears do not count twice. Manual checkpoints are unchanged by normal play or closing the app. Save failures use the visible error notice. Local JSON cannot prevent editing or reloading earlier checkpoints.

The completion transaction runs after combat for the tick. Simultaneous player/Keeper death cannot unlock the badge. Unlock and notification are saved together with the encounter. The queued notification appears on the title screen after completion, or after relaunch, avoiding the death animation and completion screen. The Achievements screen shows the permanent badge immediately. Repeated completion events do not change the unlock date or enqueue another notice.

Verification: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`. Achievement tests cover a complete ordered campaign, duplicate completion, actual damage followed by healing, immunity, environmental sources, death on the boss-death tick in both projectile orders, debug immunity, retries, practice, skipped floors, replay entry, Continue, legacy and partial evidence, and persistence of unlocked badges/notifications.
